A LITTLE TRAVEL DIARY ✿

Places I Want to Visit. ♡

From misty mountains to tropical islands, here are some beautiful places to discover in Vietnam.

01 · MOUNTAINS ✿

Ha Giang

Famous for dramatic mountain landscapes, winding roads, and peaceful villages.

02 · BEACHES ♡

Da Nang

A coastal city known for beautiful beaches, mountains, and modern city views.

03 · CULTURE 🏮

Hoi An

A charming historic city famous for colorful lanterns and old streets.

04 · ISLANDS 🌴

Phu Quoc

A tropical island destination with beaches, clear water, and nature.

05 · FLOWERS 🌸

Da Lat

A cool highland city known for flowers, gardens, and romantic scenery.

06 · RICE TERRACES 🌾

Sapa

A mountain town surrounded by beautiful valleys and rice terraces.

07 · NATURE 🏮

Ha Long Bay

Famous for limestone islands, emerald waters, and scenic boat trips.
