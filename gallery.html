MY TRAVEL NOTES

Little things to remember. ✈

Traveling isn't only about visiting beautiful places. It's also about respecting the people, culture, traditions, and environment of every destination.

  • Respect local traditions and communities.
  • Keep beaches and natural areas clean.
  • Try local food and support small businesses.
  • Take photos, but enjoy the moment too.
